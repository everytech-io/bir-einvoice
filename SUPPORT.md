# Getting help

bir-einvoice is free, open-source software maintained by one person, JE Ramos (EveryTech), in
their own time. There is no service-level agreement and no paid support. These are the places to ask, in the order that usually works best.

## Questions and ideas

* Read the [README](README.md) and the files in [docs/](docs) first.
* Search the existing [issues](https://github.com/everytech-io/bir-einvoice/issues) and
  [discussions](https://github.com/everytech-io/bir-einvoice/discussions).
* Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/everytech-io/bir-einvoice/discussions). Use "Q&A"
  for questions and "Ideas" for suggestions. Other users can answer too.

## Bugs

Open an issue with the
[bug report form](https://github.com/everytech-io/bir-einvoice/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
Say what you expected and what happened, and how to reproduce it.

## Security problems

Never in a public issue or discussion. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting, described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Keep private data out of public places

Issues and discussions are public. Do not post TINs, customer names or addresses, real invoices,
BIR credentials, application keys, signing keys, tokens or screenshots that show them. Use
made-up values.

## What we cannot help with

* **Tax, legal or compliance advice.** We cannot tell you what you must issue, when, or how to
  register. Ask your Revenue District Office, a tax professional, or the BIR. The BIR's own
  documents are the authority.
* **BIR systems.** Problems with the EIS Certification Portal, your account, your credentials, or
  a BIR decision belong with the BIR. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the BIR.
* **Your computer, network or accounts.**

## The EveryInvoice app

EveryInvoice, a free e-invoicing app by EveryTech built on this library:
https://everyinvoice.everytech.io. Questions and bug reports about the app go to
contact@everytech.io, not to this repository's issues.

## Other contact

For anything that does not belong in a public thread, write to contact@everytech.io. Use that
address for Code of Conduct reports and as a fallback for security reports. We cannot promise an
answer time.
